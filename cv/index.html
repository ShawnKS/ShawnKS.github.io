Research Interests

  • Representation
    • Learning useful representations of tabular, temporal, graph, and molecular data, through multi-task learning, space calibration, and multimodal embedding.
  • Generation
    • Modeling graph edits and molecular geometry with diffusion and flow-based methods, from conformer generation and fluid dynamics to single-step retrosynthesis.
  • Reasoning & planning
    • Combining LLMs with explicit graph and tree structures to explore reasoning paths, reuse intermediate results, and build executable synthesis routes.
